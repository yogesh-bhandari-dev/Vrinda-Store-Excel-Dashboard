# Vrinda Store: Sales Dashboard 2022

An interactive sales dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** to analyze Vrinda Store's 2022 sales across months, categories, channels, states and customer segments.

![Dashboard](dashboard.png)

## Tools & Features Used
- Excel Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- Slicers (Month, Category, Channel) for interactive filtering
- Data cleaning and formatting
- KPI cards, combo chart, pie/donut charts, bar and column charts

## Key Metrics
- **Total Sales:** ₹2,11,76,377
- **Total Orders:** 31,047
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 31,237

## Key Insights
- **Women drive the business:** Women contribute 64% of sales vs 36% for men. Adult women are the biggest segment at 34.6% of orders.
- **Top states:** Maharashtra leads in sales (₹29.9L), followed by Karnataka (₹26.4L) and Uttar Pradesh (₹21.0L).
- **Channels:** Amazon brings 35% of orders, Myntra 23% and Flipkart 22%.
- **Order status:** 93% of orders were delivered; returns (4%), cancellations (2%) and refunds (1%) are low.
- **Sales trend:** Sales and orders peaked in March and declined gradually through the year.

## Files
- `Vrinda_Store_Dashboard.xlsx`: Excel workbook (Dashboard, Pivot Tables, Clean Data, Raw Data)

## How to Use
Download the `.xlsx` file and open it in Excel. Use the slicers on the left to filter by Month, Category and Channel.
